What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the site to function; others help the owner understand how the site is used. This site also uses local storage, which works similarly — where we say “cookies” below, we mean both.

What this site does not use

We do not currently run advertising cookies, third-party marketing pixels, or cross-site tracking. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting them.

Third-party links

The footer links to external AI assistants, and our social profiles link to third-party platforms. Those sites set their own cookies under their own policies — this policy covers only artintele.ai.

Turning cookies off

Use the button above to withdraw consent for non-essential cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; note that blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.
