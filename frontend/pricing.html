How much does an AI receptionist cost in India?

A human receptionist in India typically costs ₹15,000–25,000 per month plus benefits, for one shift in one or two languages. An Artintele AI agent works 24/7 across many calls and languages, priced to your call volume and needs — so most businesses pay less than a single full-time hire. Is an AI agent cheaper than hiring a receptionist or telecaller?

For most small and mid-sized businesses, yes. One human covers a single shift and one call at a time, with training and attrition costs. An AI agent answers every call 24/7, in parallel, with no leave or turnover — so it usually does more for less than the loaded cost of an equivalent human team.

Why doesn't Artintele publish fixed prices?

Because the right setup — and price — depends on your call volume, channels, languages, integrations, and how customized the agent needs to be. Fixed packages would either overcharge simple needs or underserve complex ones. We scope to your actual requirements and send a clear quote within one business day.

What affects the price of an AI voice agent?

Five things: how many calls and messages you handle, which channels you use (voice, WhatsApp, chat), how many languages you need, what systems we integrate with (CRM, calendar), and how much custom voice and workflow design is involved. More volume and complexity, higher cost — but always scoped to you.

How do I get a price quote from Artintele?

Can an AI agent really replace a human receptionist or salesperson?

For repetitive, high-volume work — answering calls, booking appointments, qualifying leads, answering FAQs — yes, end to end. Your team then handles the exceptions and the high-value conversations. Most clients use AI to cover 24/7 and overflow that a human team could never staff cost-effectively.
