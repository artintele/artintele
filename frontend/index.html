/ FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an AI telecaller?

An AI telecaller is a human-like voice agent that makes and answers phone calls automatically — qualifying leads, booking appointments and handling support 24/7. Artintele.ai builds custom AI telecallers that speak your customers' language and integrate with your CRM, so no call ever goes unanswered.

Which languages do your AI agents support?

Our AI voice agents and chatbots can be customized to virtually any language or regional dialect. We commonly deploy in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam, and tailor a branded voice to whichever languages your customers actually speak.

How quickly does an AI voice agent answer calls?

Instantly. Artintele.ai voice agents pick up on the first ring, 24/7, with no hold time — handling inbound and outbound calls simultaneously at scale, so every lead is engaged the moment they reach out, day or night.

Will WhatsApp automation get my number banned?

Not when it's done correctly. We build WhatsApp automation on the official WhatsApp Business Platform with compliant, opt-in messaging flows — not grey-market tools. This keeps your number in good standing while qualifying leads, sending updates and replying instantly at scale.

What does Artintele.ai build?

Artintele.ai is a full-service AI automation company. We build AI voice agents, AI telecallers, receptionists, chatbots, WhatsApp automation, workflow automation, knowledge assistants, custom AI tools and CRM integrations — designed around your existing processes and deployed into your live operations end to end.

Which industries do you serve?

We work across healthcare, education, real estate, manufacturing, finance and retail — automating appointment booking, lead qualification, customer support, document processing and more. Because every solution is custom-built, we adapt the same AI capabilities to almost any sector.

How long does it take to deploy an AI solution?

It depends on scope, but our process is fast: discovery, workflow analysis, solution design, development, deployment, optimization and maintenance. Many focused automations go live in a few weeks, and we keep tuning them after launch. Book a consultation for a timeline.
