# How We Work

From idea to deployed, in seven clear steps.

1. **Discovery** — we learn your business and goals.
2. **Workflow Analysis** — we map the processes worth automating.
3. **Solution Design** — we architect the right agents and flows.
4. **Development** — we build and integrate with your stack.
5. **Deployment** — we launch into your live operations.
6. **Optimization** — we measure, tune and improve continuously.
7. **Maintenance** — we keep systems patched, monitored and reliably running.

Many focused automations go live in a few weeks. [Book a consultation for a timeline](https://artintele.ai/contact).
